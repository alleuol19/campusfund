About & FAQ

What CampusFund is, how it works, and answers to common questions.

About the system

CampusFund is St. Clare College's Online Campus Fund Raiser System — a place for students, clubs, and departments to propose fundraising causes and for the community to give directly, with every step tracked from proposal to payout.

How do I create an account?

Click "Sign up" in the top right and register using your @scc.edu school email, student ID, and password.

How do I create a fundraiser?

Sign in, go to "Create," fill out the form, and attach a letter of proposal. It goes to the Admin Desk for approval before it appears publicly.

How do I donate?

Open any fundraiser and click "Donate Now." Choose an amount (minimum ₱50) and a simulated payment method, then confirm.

Are the payments real?

No — payments are simulated for demonstration purposes. No real money moves through this system.

Terms & Conditions

Please review before creating an account

1. Acceptance of Terms

By creating an account on CampusFund, you agree to be bound by these Terms & Conditions and to use the platform in good faith for legitimate campus fundraising purposes only.

2. Eligibility

Accounts are limited to currently enrolled students, faculty, and recognized campus organizations of St. Clare College with a valid @scc.edu email address and student ID.

3. Account Responsibilities

You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for all activity that occurs under your account. Notify the Admin Desk immediately if you suspect unauthorized access.

4. Fundraiser Proposals & Approval

Every fundraiser must include an accurate description, purpose, target amount, deadline, and a signed letter of proposal. Submissions are reviewed by administrators and may be approved, rejected, or asked for revision before appearing publicly.

5. Donations

All donations processed through this platform are simulated for demonstration purposes only. No real payment is transmitted, and no real funds are held, transferred, or disbursed by this system.

6. Prohibited Conduct

You may not use CampusFund to solicit funds for a cause you do not have the authority to represent, to misrepresent how funds will be used, or to harass, defraud, or mislead donors.

7. Data & Privacy

Information you provide (name, student ID, contact details, donation history) is used only to operate the platform and is not sold or shared with outside parties.

8. Termination

Accounts found to violate these terms may be suspended or disabled, and associated fundraisers may be suspended or removed, at the discretion of the Admin Desk.

9. Changes to These Terms

These terms may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the platform after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

10. Contact

Questions about these terms can be directed to the Student Affairs Office at fundraise@scc.edu.
