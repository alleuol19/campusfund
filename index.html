About & FAQ

What CampusFund is, how it works, and answers to common questions.

About the system

CampusFund is St. Clare College's Online Campus Fund Raiser System — a place for organizers to propose fundraising causes and for contributors to give directly, with every step tracked from proposal to payout.

How do I create an account?

Click "Sign up," choose Organizer/Creator or Contributor/Donor, then register with your @scc.edu school email and student ID.

Can a contributor create a fundraiser?

No — only Organizer/Creator accounts can start a fundraiser. Contributor/Donor accounts can browse and donate.

Can an organizer donate?

No — organizer accounts cannot make donations to any fundraiser, including their own.

How do I donate?

Sign in as a Contributor/Donor, open any fundraiser, click "Donate Now," choose an amount (minimum ₱50) and a simulated payment method, then confirm.

What is a donor audit sheet?

Under "My Fundraisers," organizers can print a spreadsheet-style report of every donation to their fundraiser — with a summary, a chart, and an option to hide donor names. Contributors cannot access this.

Are the payments real?

No — payments are simulated for demonstration purposes. No real money moves through this system.

Terms & Conditions

Please review before creating an account

1. Acceptance of Terms

By creating an account on CampusFund, you agree to be bound by these Terms & Conditions and to use the platform in good faith for legitimate campus fundraising purposes only.

2. Eligibility

Accounts are limited to currently enrolled students, faculty, and recognized campus organizations of St. Clare College with a valid @scc.edu email address and student ID.

3. Account Types

At signup you choose an account type. Organizer/Creator accounts may create and manage fundraisers but may not make donations. Contributor/Donor accounts may browse and donate but may not create fundraisers.

4. Account Responsibilities

You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for all activity that occurs under your account. Notify the Admin Desk immediately if you suspect unauthorized access.

5. Fundraiser Proposals & Approval

Every fundraiser must include an accurate description, purpose, target amount, deadline, and a signed letter of proposal. Submissions remain hidden from the public board until an administrator approves them.

6. Donations & Records

Every fundraiser receives a unique campaign reference number, and every donation receives a unique donation reference number. All donations processed through this platform are simulated for demonstration purposes only.

7. Donor Audit Sheets

Organizers may generate a printable audit sheet listing every donation made to their own fundraiser, including a summary and chart, with the option to mask donor names while keeping reference numbers intact for verification. This report is only accessible to the fundraiser's creator.

8. Prohibited Conduct

You may not use CampusFund to solicit funds for a cause you do not have the authority to represent, to misrepresent how funds will be used, or to harass, defraud, or mislead donors.

9. Data & Privacy

Information you provide (name, student ID, contact details, donation history) is used only to operate the platform and is not sold or shared with outside parties.

10. Termination

Accounts found to violate these terms may be suspended or disabled, and associated fundraisers may be suspended or removed, at the discretion of the Admin Desk.

11. Contact

Questions about these terms can be directed to the Student Affairs Office at fundraise@scc.edu.
